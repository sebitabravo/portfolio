5 your arco rights (chile)

Chilean law protects your personal data. Under Law 19.628 (currently in force) and Law 21.719 (effective December 1, 2026), you have the following ARCO rights:

  • Access request information about what personal data we are processing
  • Rectification request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Cancellation request deletion of your personal data
  • Opposition object to the processing of your personal data

Since this site does not actively collect personal data, we do not maintain visitor records that can be associated with an identified or identifiable person. If you believe your data has been processed in a manner contrary to law, please contact us at the email address in Section 1 and we will resolve your request promptly.

6 your gdpr rights (european visitors)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) grants you the following rights:

  • Access request information about what personal data we process
  • Rectification request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Erasure request deletion of your personal data
  • Restriction restrict the processing of your data
  • Portability receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Objection object to the processing of your personal data
  • Withdraw consent withdraw any consent given at any time
  • Lodge a complaint file a complaint with your local data protection authority

Legal basis: this site performs no personal-data processing — no analytics, cookies, or visitor records — so there is no processing to ground in a legal basis. If you email us, your message is handled solely to respond to your request.
