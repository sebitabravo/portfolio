- **L8** (worker, evidence, S4 — owner verbatim: "el sonido esta mal seteado no funciona como deberia o sea no tiene logica"): the five confirmed sound defects are fixed with the design grammar intact (voices, gains, `sb-sound`, copy unchanged). Root causes: (1) `toggle.ts` called `sound.set(true)` → `unlock()` with a non-awaited `ctx.resume()` and then `play()`, which returns early while `ctx.state !== "running"`, so the ON press never sounded, and turning OFF was silent because `play()` sees `enabled === false`; (2) `unlock()` set `data-audio="ready"` at context *creation* even while suspended; (3) the first-gesture listeners were `{ once: true }` and consumed, so nothing resumed the context iOS suspends on hide; (4) `stage.ts` hover `pointerenter` had no pointer-type check, so a tap fired `tick` + `shutter`; (5) a failed `resume()` was never retried on a later gesture. New state machine: `<html data-audio>` is `idle` until an attempt, `ready` only when `ctx.state === "running"`, `blocked` when `resume()` cannot deliver; a `statechange` listener keeps it honest and demotes any drop out of `running` back to `idle`; `unlock(): Promise<boolean>` awaits `resume()` and records the real state; a permanent capture-phase `pointerdown`/`keydown` re-unlocks when enabled and not running; `visibilitychange` to hidden calls `ctx.suspend()` and to visible re-marks `idle`; `play()` fire-and-forget-resumes when suspended, stays silent without a prior gesture, keeps the 40 ms per-voice limit and can no longer throw; `toggle.ts` paints immediately then repaints after the await — ON awaits `unlock()` before playing `press`, OFF plays `press` before disabling, and with blocked audio the persisted preference stays `aria-checked="true"` while the label refuses to claim "on"; stage hover ticks only for `pointerType === "mouse"`.
